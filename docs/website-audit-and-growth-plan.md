# Website Audit And Growth Plan

Audit update: 29 May 2026  
Scope: Gym Vibe Cafe website after pre-Square-launch polish pass.

## Improvements Made

- Homepage hero tightened with clearer menu, directions, phone and Instagram actions.
- Added clearer first-screen trust signals for location, food availability and checkout readiness.
- Menu browser kept app-style category navigation with stronger selected states on desktop and mobile.
- Menu cards improved for scanning, popular labels, macros and future basket review.
- Checkout surface changed from live-sounding checkout to a disabled planning basket.
- Square API route now requires explicit activation before it can create checkout links.
- Location section improved with clearer address, phone, Instagram and directions presentation.
- Meal-prep copy made more cautious and confirms meal prep is not connected to ordering.
- Gallery placeholders made more intentional and premium without fake photography.
- FAQ updated to state online checkout is not live.
- Privacy, footer and checkout status copy updated for Square readiness.
- README rewritten around current status, disabled checkout and launch workflow.
- Added dedicated Square readiness documentation.

## Remaining Website Improvements

- Supply approved real food, drink, interior and exterior photography.
- Supply a higher-resolution logo and approved favicon/social preview assets.
- Confirm official production domain.
- Configure real Search Console verification after the domain is live.
- Confirm whether the Google rating snapshot should remain visible long term.
- Test sticky mobile actions, menu tabs and planning basket on real iPhone and Android devices.
- Validate production metadata, sitemap, robots and structured data after deployment.

## Checkout Readiness

Checkout is not live. The planning basket is useful for reviewing a future ordering flow, but customers should call or ask in store while Square checkout is being finalised.

Before Square can go live:

- Owner must approve online checkout or click-and-collect.
- Prices and orderable items must be confirmed.
- Square sandbox credentials must be supplied securely.
- Sandbox orders must be tested.
- Staff handling process must be confirmed.
- Production credentials must be supplied securely.
- Live test payment must be completed.

## Owner Tasks

- Confirm opening hours.
- Confirm menu items, prices, macros and availability.
- Confirm meal-prep wording, prices and availability.
- Supply approved real photography.
- Confirm official website domain.
- Supply Google Search Console verification.
- Supply official Google Business Profile link.
- Supply approved Google reviews link if review CTA should be shown.
- Decide whether Square checkout should become true click-and-collect.
- Approve final wording before launch.

## Local SEO Ideas

- Keep Google Business Profile name, address, phone, hours and website URL consistent with the site.
- Add the live website menu URL to Google Business Profile after launch.
- Upload real exterior, interior, menu-board, dish and drink photos.
- Correct conflicting external directory listings where controllable.
- Ask satisfied customers for honest reviews using an approved Google review link.
- Keep references to Kinning Park and Glasgow Southside natural, not repetitive.

## Marketing Ideas

- Add a QR code at the counter that points to `/menu` after the domain is live.
- Use Instagram posts and stories to point customers to `/menu` and `/location`.
- Create seasonal menu highlights only when prices and availability are confirmed.
- Promote meal prep only when current options and collection details are confirmed.
- Consider approved local collaborations after the website, Business Profile and photos are ready.

## Post-Launch Growth Plan

- Review Search Console and anonymous analytics monthly.
- Refresh menu content when prices or availability change.
- Add genuine photography gradually, starting with popular dishes and exterior/interior shots.
- Review mobile usability after real customer feedback.
- Add click-and-collect only after Square readiness is complete.
- Keep privacy wording aligned with any future tracking or checkout changes.
